Help from Wamballa Games, the developer of Alpha Attack.

Contact us

Email wamballagamessupport@gmail.com. This opens your email app; you choose what to send. There is no contact form on this website.

Please include your device model, Android or iOS version, the game version and build number from Settings, and a description of what happened. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending. Please leave out passwords, payment details and security codes.

Parents and guardians are welcome to contact us on a child’s behalf. Please avoid sending a child’s name, age or other personal details unless necessary to explain the issue.

Quick answers

How do I play?

Trace the letters on the dial to complete falling words before they fill the play area. Complete words and use bombs to keep the stack under control.

Where are the sound settings?

Open Settings on the title screen or pause during a run. Music and Sound Effects have separate switches. Also check your device’s volume.

How do I reset my best score?

Choose Reset Best Score in Settings. Scores and preferences are saved locally on your device.

How does Continue work?

When a rewarded ad is available after Game Over, you can choose Continue · Watch Ad. A completed reward allows one continue in that run. You can choose Play Again without watching an ad. The offer may be unavailable if an ad cannot load or if you have already attempted a continue.

How do I change advertising privacy choices?

Use Ad Privacy Choices in Settings or Pause when that control is available. Its availability depends on Google’s privacy requirements. Our planned release ad configuration uses child-directed treatment for everyone; we do not collect ages to enable personalised ads.

Privacy questions

Use the support email for privacy questions or information requests. Our privacy policy explains game data, advertising and support correspondence.
