1. Who is responsible

Steve Tyrell, trading as Wamballa Games, is an individual based in the United Kingdom and is responsible for the information described in this policy. “We”, “us” and “our” mean that individual trading as Wamballa Games.

For support or privacy questions, email wamballagamessupport@gmail.com.

2. Information in the game

Alpha Attack saves your best score, music and sound preferences on your device. Internal playtest builds also save the selected difficulty. Playing does not require an account, name, email address, date of birth or age. We do not operate a cloud-save or score-upload service.

You can reset your best score in Settings. Clearing app data removes locally stored game data. Device backups and restores depend on your operating system and its settings.

3. Optional rewarded advertisements

When available after Game Over, you may choose to watch an advertisement for one continue. You can choose Play Again without watching. We use Google AdMob to deliver rewarded advertisements and Google’s User Messaging Platform to handle applicable privacy choices.

The release configuration applies child-directed treatment to every ad request, limits ad content to the G rating, disables advertising personalisation and disables Google’s publisher first-party identifier. We do not collect ages to switch adults into personalised advertising. These settings limit advertising data use; they do not mean that no technical information is processed.

Google’s advertising SDK may process IP addresses, device or app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. The Android configuration removes Advertising ID permission, and the iOS configuration does not request permission to access IDFA. The game does not request precise location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services.

4. Children and families

Alpha Attack’s intended audience includes children under 13. The same child-directed advertising restrictions apply to everyone, without an age screen or collection of dates of birth. The game has no player accounts, chat or public player profiles.

Parents and guardians can contact us with questions or requests about a child’s information. Please avoid sending a child’s personal details unless necessary for the request. We do not use support messages to build advertising profiles or send marketing.

5. Your advertising privacy choices

Where Google requires a privacy-options control, Ad Privacy Choices appears inside Settings or the in-game Pause menu. Google’s privacy system determines whether a message or control is applicable. Child and under-age treatment may mean that an adult-style advertising consent form is not shown.

You can use available controls and your device’s privacy settings to manage applicable choices. Changing a choice affects future processing under the providers’ policies; it does not automatically erase information already held by a provider.

6. Unity technology

Alpha Attack uses the Unity game engine. Unity technology and enabled services may process technical device, application, usage or diagnostic information. Unity describes its processing and available choices in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

7. When you contact support

We receive your email address, any name included in your email, and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue and keep an appropriate record. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

For ordinary support, our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in helping players and resolving issues while respecting their rights. We rely on legal obligations where necessary to handle privacy requests or other legal duties. Our support mailbox uses Google Gmail under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

8. When you visit this website

These support and privacy pages are static. They have no added analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms or external font embeds. GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement and its Pages data-collection disclosure.

External links take you to services with their own privacy practices.

9. Sharing and retention

Google, Unity and GitHub handle information as described above and under their own policies. Their services operate internationally and may process information outside the United Kingdom. Their policies describe applicable transfer safeguards and retention practices.

Local scores and preferences remain until reset, cleared or affected by device backups. We delete support correspondence and attachments from our mailbox within 12 months of closing an enquiry, except relevant information needed for an ongoing dispute or legal obligation. Provider backups or service records may remain longer under the provider’s policies. This support-email period does not apply to advertising data or GitHub hosting logs.

We may disclose relevant information where needed to meet a legal obligation or deal with a legal claim.

10. Privacy questions and requests

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to processing, or receive certain information in portable form. Email wamballagamessupport@gmail.com to ask about information we hold. We may request information reasonably needed to confirm identity and understand the request.

For information held directly by a provider, its policy explains relevant controls and contact methods. You can also contact the UK Information Commissioner’s Office through ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or your local data-protection authority.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game or our handling of information changes and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate.
